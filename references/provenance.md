# Provenance

Super Grill is an original orchestration skill inspired by two MIT-licensed
projects. It does not require either project at runtime.

## Upstream references

### Superpowers

- Repository: <https://github.com/obra/superpowers>
- Local research revision: `44c9b2d6e889982ac18c27d05a19fefe335194e1`
- Copyright: Copyright (c) 2025 Jesse Vincent
- License: MIT
- Patterns studied: mandatory workflow routing, reconnaissance, brainstorming
  gates, written specifications, bite-sized plans, test-first implementation,
  review, and evidence-before-completion.

### Matt Pocock Skills

- Repository: <https://github.com/mattpocock/skills>
- Local research revision: `2ab958093e83e0ec752e6c1c5932da465bf23e0c`
- Copyright: Copyright (c) 2026 Matt Pocock

  decisions, and the experimental frontier-based `batch-grill-me`.

## Local research clones

Development checkouts may keep shallow upstream clones under `.upstream/`.
That directory is research material and is not part of the distributable
skill. Refresh it deliberately and update the revisions above when upstream
behavior is re-evaluated.
