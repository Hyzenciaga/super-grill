projects. It does not require either project at runtime.

## Super Grill terminology

Runtime protocol names are deliberately original parody labels rather than
copies of upstream skill names:

- Menu Inquisition and Frontier Buffet cover dependency-aware interviewing.
- Recipe Deposition and Skewer Bureaucracy cover design and planning.
- Kitchen Quarantine and the Sous-Chef Conveyor Belt cover isolated execution.
- Raw–Sizzle–Rest and Smoke Autopsy cover tests and debugging.
- Health Inspector Carousel, Thermometer Court, and Closing-time Tribunal cover
  review, verification, and branch disposition.

The renamed protocols preserve broad workflow ideas, not upstream wording.

## Local research clones

Development checkouts may keep shallow upstream clones under `.upstream/`.
That directory is research material and is not part of the distributable
skill. Refresh it deliberately and update the revisions above when upstream
behavior is re-evaluated.
